Privacy Policy

Last updated October 14, 2026

Vellix is a free Discord bot, website, and dashboard run by an independent developer in the United States ("we", "us"). This policy explains what Vellix collects, why, and the choices you have. In short: Vellix stores only what its features need, never stores the text of your chat messages, never sells data, and does not track you.

2. Message content

Vellix reads messages as they are sent but does not save their text.

  • Leveling only notes that you sent a message, so it can award XP.
  • AutoMod, when a server turns it on, checks messages for spam, links, invites, mass mentions, and blocked words. Recent message times are kept in memory for a few seconds to detect spam, then discarded.
  • Custom commands and auto-replies compare a message with that server's triggers and reply if it matches. The message itself is not stored.
  • Commands by mention. A message that starts by mentioning Vellix (like "@Vellix balance") is read as a command and answered. It is not stored.
  • AFK and highlights check each message for mentions of people who are AFK and for words people chose to highlight. This happens in memory and the message is not stored.
  • Server logs and mod logs, when a server turns them on, post copies of edited or deleted messages, and messages removed by AutoMod, into a channel in that same server. Those copies live in Discord, are controlled by that server's staff, and are not stored by Vellix.

3. The dashboard

Server staff can manage Vellix on its web dashboard after logging in with Discord.

  • Login. Discord login uses the "identify" permission only. Vellix receives your Discord user ID, name, and avatar, so it can check which servers you manage and show who is logged in. It cannot see your email, servers you don't share with Vellix, or your messages.
  • Session cookie. After login, a signed cookie named vellix_session keeps you logged in for up to 7 days, or until you log out. It holds your user ID, name, avatar link, and security codes that protect forms. It is not stored in Vellix's database. See the Cookie Policy.
  • Changes you make are saved as that server's settings (section 1). Messages sent with the message sender are posted to Discord and not stored by Vellix.

4. The website

This website has no accounts, forms, ads, analytics, or tracking, and it sets no cookies. It loads nothing from other companies: fonts and images come from this site.

  • Theme choice. If you pick light or dark mode, your browser remembers it in local storage (vellix-theme). It never leaves your device.
  • Hosting. The site is hosted by Render. Like any web host, Render may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses and browser types, to run and protect its service. See Render's Privacy Policy.
  • Do Not Track. Vellix doesn't track people across sites, so it has nothing to change when your browser sends a Do Not Track signal.

5. How data is used and shared

Data is used only to run Vellix's features. We never sell it, rent it, or use it for advertising.

  • Global leaderboards show your username, level, and coin total to users in every server that has Vellix. You can hide your name (section 7).
  • Server leaderboards show members' display names and levels to that server.
  • Server staff can see warnings, settings, and custom commands for their own server only.
  • Highlights. When someone says a word you highlight, Vellix DMs you that message and up to four messages before it, with a link. You only get messages from channels you can already read.
  • AFK. Your AFK status text is shown in the server where you set it when someone mentions you.
  • Legal requests. We may share data if the law requires it, or to protect users or Vellix from harm.

Vellix runs through Discord, so Discord also processes this data under the Discord Privacy Policy.

6. Storage, security, and retention

  • Vellix's data is kept in a private database on a computer controlled by the developer in the United States. Only the developer can access it, and the bot's login token is kept private. If you use Vellix from outside the United States, your data is processed there.
  • Levels and coins are kept while you use Vellix, so your progress is still there when you come back.
  • Warnings are kept until server staff delete them with /delwarn or /clearwarnings, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Server settings and custom commands are kept after Vellix leaves a server, so they return if it is added again. Server owners can ask us to delete them.
  • AFK statuses are deleted when you next send a message in that server. Highlights are kept until you remove them with /highlights remove or /highlights clear. Reminders are deleted as soon as they are sent or cancelled. All three are deleted for a server when Vellix leaves it.

No system is perfectly secure. If a data breach affecting users ever happens, we will announce it in the support server.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Hide your name on global leaderboards at any time with /lbprivacy. You will show as "Anonymous" instead.
  • See your data. Ask for a copy of the data Vellix stores about you.
  • Correct or delete it. Deleting it resets your levels, XP, and coins permanently. Warnings may be kept if a server needs them for moderation, but you can ask that server's staff to remove them.
  • Server owners can turn features off, or remove Vellix and ask us to delete their server's settings.

These rights apply to everyone, wherever you live. If you are in the EU or UK, we process your data because running the features you use is in our and your legitimate interests (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), you can also object to processing, and you can complain to your local data protection authority.

To make a request, contact the developer in the support server (section 9). We respond within 30 days and may ask you to confirm the request from your Discord account.

8. Children

Vellix is only for people who meet Discord's minimum age in their country, which is at least 13. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a child's data is stored, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when Vellix changes. We will update the "Last updated" date and announce major changes in the support server.

Vellix is run by an independent developer in the United States. For questions or data requests, contact the developer in the Vellix support server. See also the Terms of Service and Cookie Policy.
