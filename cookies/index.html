Cookie Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

On this page

1. On this website2. On the dashboard3. Your choices4. Changes and contact

Vellix uses no advertising, analytics, or tracking cookies, and nothing from other companies runs on its pages. The only things stored in your browser are listed below. Each one is needed for something you asked for, so there is no cookie banner to accept or decline.

1. On this website

NameTypeWhat it doesHow long
vellix-themeLocal storage (not a cookie)Remembers whether you picked light or dark modeUntil you clear your browser data

The website sets no cookies at all.

2. On the dashboard

NameTypeWhat it doesHow long
vellix_sessionCookie (strictly necessary)Keeps you logged in and protects forms from forgery. Holds your Discord user ID, name, avatar link, and security codes, signed so it can't be changed.Up to 7 days, or until you log out
vellix-themeLocal storageRemembers light or dark modeUntil you clear your browser data
vxArrive, vxLeftAmbientSession storageLets the Vellix face animation carry on smoothly between pagesDeleted when you close the tab

The dashboard only sets its cookie after you choose to log in with Discord.

3. Your choices

You can delete cookies and site data at any time in your browser settings. Deleting vellix_session logs you out of the dashboard. Deleting vellix-theme returns the site to your device's light or dark setting.

Links to Discord open Discord's own site, which has its own cookies. See the Discord Privacy Policy.

4. Changes and contact

If Vellix ever adds a cookie that isn't strictly needed, we will update this page and ask for your consent first. Questions? Contact the developer in the Vellix support server. See also the Privacy Policy.
