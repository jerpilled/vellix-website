Refund Policy

Last updated October 13, 2026

Vellix is free. There are no paid features, subscriptions, or purchases, so there is nothing to refund today.

1. Coins, XP, and games

Vellix coins, XP, and levels can't be bought, sold, or exchanged for money, and games use virtual coins only. Coins lost in games, reset for cheating, or lost to a bug have no cash value and can't be refunded. See section 4 of the Terms of Service.

2. Donations

We may one day accept voluntary donations to help keep Vellix running. If we do:

  • Donations are gifts. They don't buy features, coins, XP, levels, or any advantage.
  • Donations are not refundable, except for duplicate charges, payments you didn't authorize, or where the law requires a refund.
  • To ask about one of those, contact the developer in the Vellix support server within 30 days of the payment. If a refund is due, it goes back to the original payment method.
  • This page will be updated with the payment provider's details before donations open.

3. Your rights

Nothing in this policy limits any refund rights you have under the consumer laws where you live.

4. Contact

Vellix is run by an independent developer in the United States. Questions? Contact the developer in the Vellix support server.
