Card shop

Six looks for the wallet card /balance shows. Pick one to see it on the example card. Sign in with Discord and the dashboard draws your card in every style, where Embers unlock the ones you want.

Sign in with Discord to buy

Opens the dashboard's shop. Signing in only tells Vellix your Discord user ID, name and avatar (Privacy Policy). Buying costs Embers, never money.

How Embers come

One on every 7th day of a /daily streak, one with each /weekly, now and then from /work, one every fifth global level, and a few from a slots jackpot. One Ember also trades for 2,500 coins with /embers trade.

Also in Discord

/card shop shows your card in every style, /card buy unlocks one, and /card use switches between the ones you own. Buying here or there is the same purchase.

Just for fun

Coins, Embers and card styles can't be bought, sold or cashed out, and they're not refundable, since nothing was paid for them. See the Refund Policy.
