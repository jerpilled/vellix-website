Six card styles

/balance shows a wallet card with your coins, bank and Embers. These are example cards, drawn by the same code the bot uses. Embers switch your card to a new style.

Embers come slowly: one on every 7th day of a /daily streak, one with each /weekly, now and then from /work, one every fifth global level, and a few from a slots jackpot. One Ember also trades for 2,500 coins.

Coins and Embers are just for fun. They can't be bought, sold, or cashed out, and server admins can switch games off at any time.
